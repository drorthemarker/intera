THE 8-STATION PROCESS

Click any station to watch video footage of the automated production floor in action.

Station 01
►

Nothing is measured twice.

CNC NESTING

Full sheet in, every part out, cut and labelled. The machine reads digital specifications directly from our application.

Station 02
►

Both corners. One pass.

DOUBLE MITER SAW

Both 45° corner joints cut simultaneously on unified tooling. Ensures absolute gap-free miter closures.

Station 03
►

Bonded, not assembled.

THE PRESS

Even hydraulic pressure turns individual rails, stiles, and center panel into an inseparable single unit.

Station 04
►

One kitchen. One thickness.

CALIBRATING SANDER

Calibrates every door across an entire project to identical sub-millimeter thickness with zero outliers.

Station 05
►

A sealed edge, not a strip.

MAIN EDGEBANDER

Waterproof zero-joint polyurethane bonding creating a monolithic perimeter impenetrable to steam.

Station 06
►

Built for the slim Shaker.

THE CUSTOM EDGEBANDER

Rebuilt in-house specifically to apply seamless edgebanding to ultra-narrow 3/8" Delgado shaker rails.

Station 07
►

Flip the part. Keep the reference.

SECOND CNC (BACK BORING)

Secondary machining for hinge boring and drawer runner drillings while maintaining pristine front face condition.

Station 08
►

It leaves here the way it arrives.

SECURE PACKAGING & LOGISTICS

Rigid edge corner protectors and custom palletizing ensure zero touch-ups needed before installation.

Experience the difference that six generations of craft and custom CNC engineering deliver.
