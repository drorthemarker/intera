SHAKER

A Shaker door is defined by its frame. We make three, and the difference is the width of the rails and stiles: Delgado at 3/8 in (10 mm), Marco at 3/4 in (20 mm), and Ancho at 2 in (50 mm). Same construction, three very different faces - from a frame so slim it reads as a line, to the generous profile of a classic Shaker. Every joint is machined, not assembled from mouldings.
