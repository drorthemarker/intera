ENGINEERING & SPECIFICATIONS

Every INTERA flat slab front is engineered to eliminate warping and delamination.

1. Moisture-Resistant Core

Constructed exclusively on an architectural MR core to prevent humidity absorption and dimensional drift in kitchen and bath environments.

2. Zero-Joint Edgeband

Bonded with waterproof adhesive on all four perimeter edges, leaving zero dark lines, impenetrable to steam and spills.

3. IKEA SEKTION Compatibility

Pre-bored with CNC precision for standard European frameless cabinetry and 100% plug-and-play compatibility with IKEA SEKTION hinge lines.
