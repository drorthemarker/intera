ENGINEERING TRUE METAL FRONTS

Real metal doors typically suffer from excess weight and distortion. INTERA eliminates both.

1. Lightweight Core

By bonding genuine metal faces to an engineered moisture-resistant core, we maintain standard hinge weight limits while providing authentic metal tactile feel.

2. Chemically Locked Patina

Our rusted iron surfaces are accelerated in-house to the ideal aesthetic state, then permanently sealed so the oxidation never transfers to fingers or clothes.

3. Marine-Grade 316 Stainless

Specified with true 316 austenitic stainless steel for maximum resistance against kitchen acids, fingerprints, and coastal environmental conditions.
