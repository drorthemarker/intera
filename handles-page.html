INTEGRATED MILLED HANDLES

An integrated handle milled directly into the front itself — nothing added, no hardware to install or replace, nothing to come loose. Fino and Gorda are the same profile; the only difference is the material thickness — Fino at 19 mm, Gorda at 21 mm, which gives Gorda a deeper grip. Both thicknesses are available in laminate and veneer.

WHY DOUBLE-SEALING MATTERS

The reason most integrated handles fail is water ingress at the raw cut. INTERA solves this permanently.

FINO (19 MM)

The standard 19 mm thickness profile featuring a crisp, geometric milled channel with zero hardware to buy or install. Double-sealed at the cut to permanently protect against moisture and steam. Available in laminate and veneer.

GORDA (21 MM)

Shares the exact same profile geometry as Fino on a 21 mm thickness substrate, providing an extra 2 mm depth for a deeper, more substantial finger grip purchase. Available in laminate and veneer.
