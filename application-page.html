PRECISION ORDERING WORKFLOW

How our digital interface bridges design specification directly with our 8-station factory floor.

Color Selection by Advanced Parameters

Filter through roughly 150 finishes by substrate (Synchronized Laminate, Acrylic, Veneer, Real Metal), tone, grain direction, and surface gloss. Direct visual validation ensures every front matches your rendering specifications.

Front Ordering to Exact Specifications

Input exact door, drawer front, and filler dimensions to the millimeter or fractional inch. The application calculates edge allowances, label codes, and optimal nesting patterns across full raw sheets automatically.

Precise Hinge Boring Selection

Select standard European 35mm hinge cups, custom top/bottom cup distances, or 100% plug-and-play IKEA SEKTION drilling lines. Our CNC boring heads machine both sides without manual tape measuring.

Precise Drawer Drilling Selection

Pre-programmed drilling coordinates for leading undermount and double-wall drawer systems (Blum Legrabox/Tandembox, Grass Vionaro, Hettich AvanTech, and IKEA MAXIMERA). Fast installation with zero field drilling.

Precise Lift-System (Flap) Drilling Selection

Specify overhead cabinet bi-fold and parallel lift hardware (Blum AVENTOS HF, HK-S, HL, HS). Calibrated bracket drillings ensure perfectly balanced vertical operation from the moment doors are hung.
