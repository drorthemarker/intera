ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

INTERA CABINET DOORS is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We continually improve the user experience for everyone and apply the relevant accessibility standards.

1. Conformance Status (WCAG 2.2 Level AA)

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) defines requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. It defines three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA. The INTERA website is engineered to conform with WCAG 2.2 Level AA and the requirements of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) Title III.

2. Technical Specifications & Built-in Features

Accessibility of the INTERA website relies on native HTML5 semantics and modern ARIA markup:

  • Keyboard Navigability: All interactive elements including buttons, links, swatch triggers, and modals are fully operable via standard keyboard tabs and navigation keys.
  • Visible Focus Indicators: High-contrast 2px outline focus states (outline-offset: 4px) are implemented across all focusable elements.
  • Screen Reader Semantic Structure: Distinct landmarks (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <section>, <footer>) and hierarchical headings (H1 through H4) are strictly structured.
  • Skip Navigation: A direct "Skip to main content" mechanism is active for keyboard and assistive technology users.
  • Text Contrast & Resizing: High-contrast color ratios exceeding 4.5:1 for normal text and scalable vector iconography.
  • Accessible Media: Video content is hosted behind accessible interactive controls with pause, volume, and full-screen support without autoplay audio loops.
