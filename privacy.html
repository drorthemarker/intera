Privacy & Data Governance

PRIVACY POLICY & COOKIE NOTICE

This Privacy Policy outlines how INTERA CABINET DOORS collects, uses, and safeguards information obtained from visitors and B2B trade clients, in accordance with US privacy laws including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or submit trade inquiries, we may collect business contact information (name, firm name, email address, phone number, and project specifications) voluntarily provided through our B2B smart forms.

2. Cookie Policy & Tracking Technologies

We utilize essential and analytics cookies to optimize performance, remember your cookie consent preferences, and understand website traffic patterns. You may accept or decline non-essential cookies via our consent banner at any time.

3. How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used exclusively to fulfill sample requests, prepare architectural project quotations, grant access to our digital ordering application, and communicate technical updates regarding our cabinetry collections. We do NOT sell or monetize personal or commercial data.

4. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Rights

California residents possess the right to request disclosure of categories of personal information collected, request deletion of personal information, and opt-out of any prospective sales of data without discriminatory treatment.

5. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

For inquiries concerning this policy or to exercise your privacy rights, please contact: eyal@interetz.co.il.
